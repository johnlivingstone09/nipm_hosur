    Membership

    Grow. Connect. Lead — become part of India's premier HR community.

    Join the Chapter

    Take the next step in your HR journey

    Become part of India's premier HR community and connect with professionals across the nation. Membership is processed through the national institute, after which you can engage with all Hosur Chapter activities.

    1. Visit the national membership portal at nipm.in
    2. Select your type of membership
    3. Complete and submit the application
    4. Pay the applicable membership fee
    5. Connect with the Hosur Chapter for local activities

    Member Benefits

    Networking · Learning · Careers · Events · Resources · Leadership

    Six things membership of the NIPM Hosur Chapter puts within reach.

    Networking

    Meet HR, IR and plant leaders from across the Hosur industrial belt at every programme — the relationships outlast the sessions.

    Learning

    Workshops, technical training and briefings on labour codes, compliance, engagement and organisational development.

    Careers

    Job movement across member organisations, referrals within the network, and professional standing that travels with you.

    Events

    Priority access and member rates for seminars, conclaves, industrial visits and the flagship IR Conference.

    Resources

    NIPM publications including Personnel Today, the NIPM 4000-2016 people-process standards and chapter session material.

    Leadership

    Speak at chapter forums, chair committees and stand for office — visible roles that build a professional profile.

    IR Conference 2026 — delegate registration

    Choose individual or bulk registration, complete every required field, and the total amount payable is calculated for you. The form can only be submitted with the UPI transaction ID and the payment screenshot attached.

    Pay first, then fill this form

    The UPI transaction ID / UTR number and a screenshot of the completed payment are both compulsory. Without them the registration cannot be verified and the seat cannot be held.

    1Person 1 — your details

    These are the details of the person making this registration. If you choose bulk registration, you are Person 1 and every other member of your group is entered further down the form.
